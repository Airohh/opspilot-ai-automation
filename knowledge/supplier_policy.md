# Supplier policy

Demonstration policy for the OpsPilot portfolio. This is not a real company rule.

Supplier facts used in a recommendation must come from the supplier API.

If the supplier API has no record, say that the supplier was not found. Do not invent terms, risk, or contract status.

A supplier whose contract status is not active cannot receive a payment-term change. Ask for clarification instead.

Risk level is context for the human reviewer. It does not by itself approve or reject a request.
